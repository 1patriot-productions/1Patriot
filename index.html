About

Meet Jen Ritzie & Ritzie Custom Designs

Ritzie Custom Designs is a warm, craft-forward studio where every shirt, wreath, shadow box, and keepsake starts with your idea. Whether you need uniforms for a small business, family reunion tees, a cross for a funeral, or an epoxy resin piece that preserves a flower from a loved one’s casket — Jen creates with care, creativity, and no cookie-cutter limits.

Love patriotic designs? Prefer bold, uncensored artwork? Want something totally unique from scratch — or a simple clothing repair? You name it. Plus, explore AI design options free with purchase to spark ideas when you order.
